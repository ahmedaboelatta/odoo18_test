# Bird Connector V1.9.13

## Inbox productivity
- Added live search by Bird contact name, WhatsApp number, last message, or channel.
- Added conversation assignment to active internal Odoo users.
- Added **Take** action to assign the currently open conversation to yourself.
- Added **My Conversations** and **Unassigned** inbox filters.
- Shows assignee badges directly in the conversation list.
- Shows Bird Contact tags in the inbox list and conversation header for faster classification.
- Search, channel filters, assignments, media, Closed, Needs Reply and Unread can be used together.

## Compatibility
- Existing conversations remain unassigned until a user takes or assigns them.
- No changes to Bird Contacts, webhook URLs, channel separation, or historical message records.
